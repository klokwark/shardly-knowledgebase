# Playbook: suspected abuse

**Category:** `security` · **Escalate to a manager or admin.** Support cannot
suspend.

## What you are looking at

A server that looks like it is doing something it should not — mining, a
proxy, an attack, illegal content.

## Do not

- Accuse the customer before you know.
- Suspend without authority, or before somebody has actually looked.
- Discuss it in a public channel.
- Tell a reporter who the customer is.

## Gather

- What made you suspect it? A report, a resource pattern, a filename?
- Sustained 100% CPU on a bot with no traffic is the classic mining shape.
- What is on the disk? You can see files.
- How old is the account, and what has it done?

## Severity

**Immediate suspension, no notice** — admin, now:

- Child sexual abuse material. Suspend, preserve, report to the authorities.
  Do not contact the customer. Escalate to admin the moment you see it and do
  not handle it alone.
- An active attack on another system from our address.

**Suspend and tell them** — manager:

- Mining.
- Malware distribution.
- A bot built for harassment or raiding.

**Ask first** — anything ambiguous:

- A proxy or tunnel. Against the rules unasked, but often innocent.
- Unusual load that might be legitimate.
- A bot whose purpose is unclear from outside.

## Asking first

> We have noticed your server running at full CPU continuously, which is
> unusual for a bot. Could you tell me what it is doing? I am asking because
> sustained load like this is usually mining, which we do not allow — if it is
> something else, no problem at all, I just need to know what.

Give them a real chance to explain. We have been wrong about this.

## If we suspend

They get told why, and what to do about it. A suspension with no explanation
is how you get a furious ticket and a chargeback.

Suspension is not deletion. If it was a misunderstanding we reverse it, and
that is not an embarrassment — being correctable is the point.

## Somebody reporting a bot

Take it seriously. Get the bot's name and tag, what it did, when, and evidence
if they have it.

**Never tell the customer who reported them.**

Tell the reporter we are looking at it. Do not tell them what we did — that is
the other customer's business.
