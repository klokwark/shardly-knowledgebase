# Reply checklist

Run through this before you send. It takes fifteen seconds and catches most of
what goes wrong.

## Content

- [ ] Does the first sentence answer the question?
- [ ] Did I answer **the question they asked**, not the one I found
      interesting?
- [ ] Is there a second question further down their message I skipped?
- [ ] Are the numbers right? (Check
      [plans and entitlements](../reference/plans-and-entitlements.md) — do
      not recall from memory.)
- [ ] Am I promising anything from
      [not built yet](../reference/not-built-yet.md)?

## Specifics

- [ ] Exact button and menu names, not "the settings area"?
- [ ] Real timeframes, not "shortly"?
- [ ] If I asked for something, did I ask for **everything** I need at once?
- [ ] Linked the article rather than retyping it?

## Tone

- [ ] Used their name?
- [ ] Signed with mine?
- [ ] Any of the banned words?
      (unfortunately, kindly, please be advised, reach out, at this time)
- [ ] Have I apologised more than once?
- [ ] Does anything read as blaming them?

## Safety

- [ ] No credentials quoted back — not a token, not a password?
- [ ] No other customer's data in a log excerpt I pasted?
- [ ] If this is destructive, have I said so plainly?

## Admin

- [ ] Right status? `waiting` only if the ball is genuinely with them.
- [ ] Right priority, based on the situation and not the tone?
- [ ] Internal note for the next person?
- [ ] Assigned to me if I am working it?

## The last one

**Read it back as the customer.** Does it answer the question, tell them what
to do, and leave them knowing what happens next?

If you would be annoyed to receive it, rewrite it.
