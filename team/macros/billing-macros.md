# Billing macros

## Payment failed, server suspended

> Your subscription did not renew — the payment was declined on {date} — so
> the server is suspended.
>
> **Your files are all still there.** Nothing has been deleted.
>
> Update the card under **Billing → Manage billing** and it comes back
> straight away. If the card is fine, it is often a bank blocking an
> unexpected foreign charge; approving it once usually settles it for good.

## Cancelling

> You can cancel any time from **Billing → Manage billing → Cancel**.
>
> Your server keeps running until {date}, the end of the period you have
> already paid for — you are not cut off when you press the button.
>
> **Please download anything you want to keep before then.** Once the period
> ends the server is removed, and its backups go with it.

## Invoices

> Every invoice is under **Billing → Manage billing**, ready to download.
>
> They are issued by Polar, our merchant of record — which is why their name
> is on them rather than ours. That is normal and accepted by accountants. If
> yours needs something specific, tell me what and I will sort it.

## VAT ID after the fact

> VAT IDs are entered at checkout, and unfortunately I cannot add one to a
> subscription that already exists — it would mean changing an invoice that
> has been issued.
>
> Two options: enter it when you next renew, or I can cancel and re-create the
> subscription with it. Tell me which you would prefer.

Change "unfortunately" out before sending. It is in the banned words list for
a reason.

## Refund — gathering, not promising

> Let me get this to the person who can action it. So I have the full picture:
> you bought {plan} on {date}, and {what went wrong}. Is that right?
>
> I will come back to you {timeframe} with an answer either way.

**Never** "I will get you a refund". You do not decide that.

## Which plan should I buy

> Starter is 512 MB and one shard — plenty for a bot in a few hundred servers.
> Pro is 2 GB and three shards, which is where most bots end up once they are
> growing. Shard is 8 GB and ten shards, for sharded bots serving hundreds of
> thousands of members.
>
> Roughly: how many Discord servers is your bot in, and does it cache much?
> That usually settles it in one message.

## Discount code missed at checkout

> A code has to go in at checkout — I cannot apply one to a subscription that
> already exists, because the discount is part of how it was created.
>
> If you bought in the last few minutes I can cancel and refund it so you can
> buy again with the code. Say the word and I will do that now.
