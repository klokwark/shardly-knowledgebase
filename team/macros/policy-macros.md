## Deleting an account

> **Account → Data → Delete account**. It asks for a code from your email.
>
> Please read this before you press it: there is no grace period. Your
> servers and every file on them go immediately, and we cannot undo it —
> there is nothing left to restore from.
>
> Download anything you want to keep first. Your files from the file manager,
> and your account data from Account → Data → Export.
>
> Invoices survive for ten years because tax law requires it, and your tickets
> stay as our record with your name and email removed.

## Data export

> You can do this yourself: **Account → Data → Export data**. It gives you
> everything we hold — profile, servers, tickets — as JSON.
>
> Your activity log is separate, as CSV, from Account → Security.
>
> If you need it in another format or certified for a legal process, tell me
> what it has to look like.

## Reporting somebody else's bot

>
> Could you send the bot's name and tag, roughly when this happened, and any
> screenshots you have?
>

> account's business, but I will confirm that we have looked. We will not tell
> them who reported it.
