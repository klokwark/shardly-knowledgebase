# Buy a plan

## The plans

| | Starter | Pro | Shard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Memory | 512 MB | 2 GB | 8 GB |
| CPU | shared vCPU | 1 dedicated core | 2 dedicated cores |
| Storage | 5 GB NVMe | 20 GB NVMe | 80 GB NVMe |
| Shards included | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Backup slots | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Weekly | €2 | €5 | €12 |

Monthly billing is also available and works out about 12% cheaper than paying
weekly.

All prices exclude tax. Tax is worked out at checkout based on where you are.

## How to buy

1. Pick a plan from the pricing page or from **Deployments → Add server**.
2. Choose weekly or monthly.
3. Pay. Checkout is handled by Polar, our merchant of record.
4. You come back to Shardly and the server starts building on its own.

You do not need to fill in a form after paying. A paid plan is an instruction
to build the server, so we build it.

## One subscription, one server

Each subscription buys one server. If you want a second bot on its own server,
buy a second plan — your account can hold as many as you like, and they are
billed separately.

## Sold-out plans

A plan can show as sold out. That is real: we run our own hardware and we do
not oversell it. When a tier is sold out, the checkout button is disabled
rather than taking your money for something we cannot deliver.

Ask in Discord if you want to know when capacity is coming back.

## Discount codes

Enter the code at checkout, not afterwards. A code cannot be applied to a
subscription that already exists — you would have to cancel and buy again,
which is usually not worth it.

## Still stuck?

[Open a billing ticket](https://shardly.xyz/app/support/new). Include the
email address you paid with if it is different from your account address.
