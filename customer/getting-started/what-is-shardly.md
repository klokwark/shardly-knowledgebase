# What Shardly is

Shardly hosts Discord bots on hardware we own, in Germany, and charges by the
week.

You upload your code, we keep the process running. When it exits we start it
again. You get a console, a file editor, logs, and a dashboard that is ours
rather than a reskinned control panel.

## What you get

- **A server that stays up.** Your bot runs as a managed process. If it exits,
  we restart it.
- **A real editor.** Open a file, change a line, save, restart. No SFTP client
  required.
- **Live console and logs.** See what your bot is printing, as it prints it.
- **Sharding.** Your plan includes shards; more can be added.
- **Backups.** Two manual snapshot slots on every plan.
- **Support from people.** Tickets are answered by humans who can look at your
  server.

## What it is not

Shardly is not a general VPS. You do not get root, you cannot run arbitrary
services, and it is built around one job: keeping a bot process alive.

If you need a database beside your bot, that is an add-on. If you need
something Shardly does not do, ask before you buy — we would rather tell you
no than take the money.

## Where things run

Our own machines in Frankfurt, Falkenstein and Nuremberg. Ryzen 9 cores, NVMe
storage, and a short network hop to Discord's European gateway. Your data does
not leave the EU.

## Runtimes

Right now Shardly runs **Discord.js**. Other runtimes are listed on the
pricing page as sold out or coming, and that is literal — we do not sell
capacity we do not have.

## Still stuck?

Open a ticket from your dashboard. If you have not bought anything yet, ask in
[our Discord](https://discord.gg/shardly) instead — the same people read both.
