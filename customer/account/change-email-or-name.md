# Change your name or email

## Your display name

**Account → Profile.** Change it, save it. That is all — it is the name shown
on your tickets and in the dashboard.

## Your email address

The address field is read-only in the dashboard, and that is deliberate rather
than unfinished. Your address is what every sign-in, invoice and notification
is keyed to, so changing it is a flow with a verification in it — not a text
box.

**To change it, open a ticket** from the account that owns it. Say what the
new address is. We will verify both before we move anything.

If you have lost access to the old address entirely, say so — that is a
different and slower process, because "I cannot prove I own the old address"
is exactly what somebody taking over your account would also say.

## Your billing email

Separate from your account address, and held by Polar, our payment processor.
Change it from **Billing → Manage billing**, which opens their portal.

An invoice going to a different address from your sign-in is completely
normal, especially for a company.
