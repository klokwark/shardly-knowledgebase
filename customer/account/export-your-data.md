# Export your data

**Account → Data → Export data** downloads everything we hold about you as a
JSON file.

## What is in it

- Your profile: name, address, when you joined, which sign-in methods are
  linked
- Every server: plan, configuration, status, dates
- Every support ticket, with its messages

## What is not

- **Your password.** It is stored as a hash and it is a credential, not
  personal data.
- **Your bot token and panel password.** Same reasoning.
- **Your files.** They are on your server, where you can download them from
  the file manager. They are not copied into the export because a server's
  disk can be gigabytes.

## It asks me to confirm

Yes. An export is a complete copy of your account leaving our control, so it
asks for a code sent to your address before it runs. The request is recorded
in your [activity log](account-activity-log.md).

## The activity log itself

Exported separately, as CSV, from **Account → Security**. Different format
because a log is a table and JSON is a bad table.

## Under GDPR

This is your right of access and your right to portability, self-service and
immediate. If you need it in another format, or need it certified for a legal
process, open a ticket and say so.
