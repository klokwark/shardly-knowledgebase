# Get Discord DMs

Shardly can send notifications as a Discord DM instead of, or as well as, an
email.

## Connect it

**Account → Notifications → Connect Discord.**

You will be asked to authorise, and to join the Shardly Discord server if you
are not already in it.

## Why it wants to add me to a server

Because Discord will not let a bot message somebody it shares no server with.
There is no permission or scope that changes this — being in a server together
is the mechanism.

So connecting adds you to ours. You can mute it entirely and still get your
DMs; you just cannot leave it and keep them.

## Why is this separate from signing in with Discord?

They are two different Discord applications, on purpose.

The sign-in application proves who you are. The notification application
delivers messages. Keeping them apart means somebody who signed up with an
email address can still get DMs, and somebody who signs in with Discord is not
automatically opted in to being messaged.

The practical upshot: **signing in with Discord does not connect
notifications.** You have to do this separately, even if the account is the
same.

## Disconnecting

Same screen. Notifications fall back to email.

## It says it cannot message me

Almost always one of:

- You left the Shardly server.
- Your privacy settings block DMs from server members.
- You blocked the bot.

Fix any of those and press connect again.
