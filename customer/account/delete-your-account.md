# Delete your account

**Account → Data → Delete account.** It asks for a code sent to your address,
and then it happens.

## Read this part first

**There is no grace period.** No thirty days, no recycle bin, no "are you
sure" email the next morning. When it runs:

- Every server you own is deleted from the panel, with its files.
- Your account, your sign-in methods and your sessions are removed.
- You are signed out.

We cannot undo it. Not with a ticket, not the same hour, not at all — there is
nothing left to restore from.

**Download anything you want to keep before you press it.** Your files, from
the file manager. Your data, from
[the export](export-your-data.md).

## What outlives the account

Two things, and both are legal obligations rather than choices:

- **Invoices**, for as long as tax law requires. In Germany that is ten years.
- **Support tickets**, as our record of the conversation, with your name and
  email removed from them.

Server logs are held for 14 days and then go on their own.

## Cancelling versus deleting

Different things.

**Cancelling a subscription** stops it renewing. Your server keeps running to
the end of the period you have paid for. Your account stays.

**Deleting your account** is immediate and total.

If you want to stop paying, cancel. See
[changing or cancelling](../billing/change-or-cancel-plan.md).

## An active subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically refund what you have already
paid. If there is a lot left on the period, open a ticket *before* deleting —
afterwards there is no account to discuss it against.
