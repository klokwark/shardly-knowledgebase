# Account activity log

**Account → Security → Account activity** is a record of everything
consequential that has happened on your account.

## What is in it

- Servers created, deleted and reinstalled
- Power actions
- Files written and deleted
- Repository imports
- Plan and limit changes
- Sessions signed out
- Data exports
- **Anything our support team did to your account**

Each entry has what happened, who did it, and when.

## The staff entries

Entries where somebody at Shardly acted on your account are badged. That is
the reason this page is shown to you at all rather than kept internal — an
audit log the customer cannot read is an internal tool with a reassuring name.

If you see a staff action you did not ask for and cannot explain, ask. There
is usually a ticket next to it.

## What is not in it

Your own addresses are not shown. Neither are those of the agents who helped
you — their address is their personal data, not yours, and it is the same
field.

## Exporting

**Export as CSV** downloads the whole log, up to 10,000 entries.

Export is included on the Shard plan. On any plan, support can export it for
you — ask, and say why if it is for an audit, because that sometimes changes
what is useful to include.

## How long it is kept

Two years, then it is deleted automatically.

Nothing can edit or remove an entry, including us. That is what makes it worth
anything when there is a disagreement about what happened.
