# Where you are signed in

**Account → Security → Where you are signed in** lists every browser currently
signed in to your account, with the device, the address it connected from, and
when it was last used.

## Signing one out

Press **Sign out** on any row. That session ends immediately.

The device you are reading this on is marked **This device** and has no button
— use the ordinary sign-out for that one.

## Signing out everywhere else

One button, at the top. Use it if:

- You used a shared or public machine and are not sure you signed out.
- You lost a laptop or phone.
- You see a row you do not recognise.

Your current session survives; everything else ends.

## A row you do not recognise

Do this in order:

1. **Sign out everywhere else.**
2. **Change your password** — which also signs out other sessions, belt and
   braces.
3. **Turn on two-factor authentication** if it is not on.
4. Check your [activity log](account-activity-log.md) for anything you did not
   do.
5. Open a security ticket and say what you saw.

## About the location

It is the network address the session connected from, not a city. We do not
run addresses through a geolocation service, because that would mean sending
our customers' addresses to a third party for a nicety.

An address that looks unfamiliar is not automatically alarming — mobile
networks and VPNs move you around. An unfamiliar *device* is more telling.
