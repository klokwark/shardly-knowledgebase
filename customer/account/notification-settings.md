# Notification settings

**Account → Notifications.** A grid: what we tell you about, down the side;
how we tell you, across the top.

## What we tell you about

| Type | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Ticket replies | Support answered you |
| Billing | Payments, renewals, a subscription lapsing |
| Server status | Your server is built, or could not be |
| Announcements | Maintenance and platform news |
| Security | Sign-ins, password changes, session revocations |

**Security cannot be switched off.** If somebody changes your password, you
are going to hear about it. That is not a preference.

## How we tell you

- **Email** — on by default for everything.
- **Discord DM** — off until you connect Discord for notifications. See
  [getting Discord DMs](link-discord-for-dms.md).

## The fifteen-minute rule for ticket replies

When support answers you, we do not email immediately.

If you are on the ticket page when the reply lands, you see it there and no
email is sent. If you have not read it after fifteen minutes, we email you.

This is why a reply you already read does not also fill your inbox.

## The sound

There is a toggle for a sound when a reply arrives while you have a ticket
open. It applies to that browser only — it is a property of the device, not of
the account, so your laptop does not decide whether your phone makes a noise.

## Turning everything off

You can, except security. We would rather you kept billing on — the most
common way people lose a server is not noticing a failed payment.
