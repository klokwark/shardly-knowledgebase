# I cannot sign in

## Forgotten password

**Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The link goes to your address and
ends every session on the account when you use it.

## You signed up with Google or Discord

Then you have no password, and resetting one will not help. Use the button you
originally used.

If you cannot remember which, try each — you will know from whether it signs
you in.

## The reset email has not arrived

- Check spam.
- Wait a minute. There is a cooldown, so pressing repeatedly sends no extra
  mail.
- Confirm the address is spelled right.
- Five an hour per address is the limit. Past that, wait.

## A sign-in link says it is invalid

It is single use and it expires. Most often it was already opened — sometimes
by an email client generating a preview. Request another and open it yourself.

## Lost your two-factor device

If you have your backup codes, use one instead of the six digits.

If you do not, open a ticket. We will ask you to prove the account is yours,
and it is deliberately not instant — an account recovery that can be talked
through quickly is a vulnerability.

What helps: a recent invoice number, the id of a server on the account, the
address on file, when you signed up.

## "Your account has been suspended"

Someone at Shardly suspended it, and there will be a reason — usually an
[acceptable use](../policies/acceptable-use.md) matter or an unresolved
payment dispute.

Open a ticket from another address, or email support. We will tell you why.

## You cannot open a ticket without signing in

Correct, and that is the bind you are in. Ask in
[our Discord](https://discord.gg/shardly) instead.

**Support will never ask for your password**, on Discord or anywhere. Anyone
who does is not us.
