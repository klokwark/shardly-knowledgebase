# Server stuck building

You paid, it says Queued or Building, and it is not moving.

## Give it five minutes

A build normally takes under a minute. If the panel is briefly busy the build
retries on its own, and a retry looks exactly like being stuck.

The page updates itself — you do not need to reload.

## After five minutes

Builds retry with a growing gap between attempts. Depending on how the
retries fall, a build that hit a problem can take longer than you would like
to either succeed or give up.

**You do not need to watch it.** It carries on with the page closed, and we
email you either way.

## If it says Setup failed

We stopped trying. The page shows why and offers **Try again** — press it,
because many of these are a node that was briefly out of room.

Our team is told automatically when a build gives up, so somebody already
knows. You are welcome to open a ticket anyway if you want a person on it.

## Your plan is safe

You have paid for it and you still have it. It is the build that failed, not
the purchase. Nothing is lost by waiting or retrying.

## If you bought and see nothing at all

Not even a queued server? Then the order did not reach us. Check:

- The payment actually completed — look for the Polar receipt.
- You are signed in as the account you bought with. Paying with a different
  email address than your account is the single most common cause.

If the receipt exists and the account is right, open a ticket with the receipt
attached. We can match the order up by hand.

## Still stuck?

[Open a server ticket.](https://shardly.xyz/app/support/new) Include the
receipt if it is about a purchase.
