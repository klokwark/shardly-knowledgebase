# When something is wrong

Read the last twenty lines of your console log first. Genuinely — it is the
answer more often than any article here.

- [My bot will not start](bot-wont-start.md)
- [My bot keeps crashing](bot-keeps-crashing.md)
- [The server is running but my bot is offline](bot-offline-but-server-running.md)
- [Out of memory](out-of-memory.md)
- [Disk full](disk-full.md)
- [Dependencies will not install](dependency-install-fails.md)
- [Intents and privileged gateway](intents-and-privileged-gateway.md)
- [Rate limited by Discord](rate-limited-by-discord.md)
- [Server stuck building](server-stuck-building.md)
- [I cannot sign in](cant-sign-in.md)
- [Too many requests (429)](too-many-requests.md)

Still nothing? [Open a ticket](https://shardly.xyz/app/support/new) with your
log. Or check [is the platform down?](../policies/uptime-and-status.md).
