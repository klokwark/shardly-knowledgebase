# Invoices and VAT

## Getting an invoice

**Billing → Manage billing** opens the Polar portal. Every invoice is there to
download, whenever you like.

They arrive by email as well, to your billing address.

## Why Polar's name is on it

Polar is our merchant of record. They are the seller of record for tax
purposes, which is why the invoice carries their details and their tax
registration rather than ours.

This is normal and is accepted by every accountant we have met. If yours
objects, open a ticket and we will send what they need.

## VAT

Worked out at checkout from where you are.

**Business customers in the EU:** enter your VAT ID at checkout and the
reverse charge applies. Adding it afterwards is awkward — it is a change to an
invoice that already exists — so enter it the first time if you can.

There is not yet a field in the Shardly dashboard for a VAT ID; it is entered
at Polar's checkout. If you have already bought without one, open a ticket
rather than trying to fix it yourself.

## Purchase orders, bank transfer, annual billing

Not available self-service today. If your company needs one of them, open a
ticket and say what your finance team requires — we would rather do it by hand
than lose you to a form we have not built.
