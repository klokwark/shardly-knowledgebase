# Refunds

## The short version

**Cancelling does not refund the current period.** Your server runs to the end
of what you have paid for, and that is what the money bought.

## When we refund anyway

We are not difficult about this. Ask if:

- **We could not deliver.** You paid and the server never built, or a fault on
  our side made it unusable for a meaningful part of the period.
- **You bought the wrong thing minutes ago.** A double purchase, the wrong
  tier, a second subscription you did not mean to start. Say so quickly.
- **A tier was sold out and you were charged anyway.** That should not be
  possible, and if it happens it is our mistake.

## When we usually will not

- You changed your mind three weeks into a month.
- Your bot did not work because of your code. We will help you fix it — that
  is what support is for — but the server did what it was paid to do.
- You forgot to cancel. Ask anyway if it is the first time; we are human about
  it once.

## How to ask

[Open a billing ticket](https://shardly.xyz/app/support/new) and say what
happened. Include the invoice number if you have it.

You do not need to build a case. Tell us plainly; we will tell you plainly.

## Chargebacks

Please talk to us first.

A chargeback costs us a fee and takes weeks, and while it is open the account
is frozen — including servers you are still using. Every case we have seen
would have been settled faster by a ticket.
