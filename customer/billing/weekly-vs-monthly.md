# Weekly or monthly

## The difference

**Weekly** costs slightly more per day and lets you stop after any week.

**Monthly** is about 12% cheaper and commits you to the month you have paid
for.

Neither has a contract or a minimum term. The only real question is how much
notice you want to be able to give yourself.

## Why weekly exists at all

Most hosts bill monthly because it suits them. A bot you are still deciding
about should not cost you a month to find out, so weekly is the default and
the headline price.

## Switching

Cancel the current subscription and buy the other. There is no in-place
switch, because the two are separate products at Polar.

Do it in this order:

1. Buy the new plan first, if you can afford the overlap — then there is no
   gap.
2. Or wait until the current period is nearly over, cancel, and buy again.

If a gap would mean your bot going offline at a bad moment, open a ticket and
we will work around it.

## Which is cheaper, exactly?

For Starter, weekly is €2 — call it €8.70 a month at 4.35 weeks. Monthly comes
out around 12% under that. Same ratio on the other tiers.
