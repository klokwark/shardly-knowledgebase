# Change or cancel your plan

## Cancelling

**Billing → Manage billing → Cancel.**

Your server keeps running until the end of the period you have already paid
for. You are not cut off the moment you press the button, and you are not
refunded for the remainder either — see [refunds](refunds.md).

When the period ends the server is suspended, then removed.

**Download your files before then.** Once a server is removed the files are
gone, and we cannot get them back.

## Upgrading or downgrading

There is no in-place change today. To move between tiers:

1. Buy the new plan. You get a second, empty server.
2. Move your files across — download from one, upload to the other, or import
   the repository again.
3. Move your token and variables across.
4. Cancel the old subscription.

Do it in that order and your bot is only down for the restart.

If the files are large or you would rather we did it, open a ticket. We can
usually move a server between tiers by hand, and would rather do that than
have you lose data to a botched copy.

## Pausing

Not a thing we offer. A paused server still occupies the hardware it is on, so
there is nothing to discount.

If you are stopping for a month, take a backup, download it, and cancel.

## Cancelling versus deleting your account

Cancelling stops a subscription. [Deleting your
account](../account/delete-your-account.md) removes everything, immediately
and irreversibly. They are very different buttons.
