Monthly billing is about 12% cheaper than weekly. Prices exclude tax.

## Which one?

**Starter** if your bot is in a handful of servers and does not hold much in
memory. One shard covers roughly 2,000 Discord servers.

**Pro** for a bot that has outgrown that: more members, more commands, a
dependency tree that needs room.

**Shard** for sharded bots serving hundreds of thousands of members.

You are not locked in. If you pick wrong, change it — see
[changing your plan](change-or-cancel-plan.md).

## Sold out

We run our own hardware and do not oversell it, so a tier can genuinely be
sold out. When it is, checkout is disabled rather than taking money for
something we cannot deliver.

## Build your own

The pricing page has a calculator if none of the three fit. The number it
shows is the number you pay — no setup fee, no bandwidth bill, no per-request
charge, and no price that doubles at renewal.

## Still stuck?

Ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/shardly) before you buy. We would rather
talk you out of the wrong plan than refund it later.
