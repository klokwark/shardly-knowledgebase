# Privacy, in short

The full policy is at [shardly.xyz/privacy](https://shardly.xyz/privacy). This
is the version you can read in two minutes. Where they disagree, the policy
wins.

## What we hold

- **Your account.** Email address, a hashed password if you have one, your
  display name, and the identifiers of any Google, Discord or GitHub account
  you connect.
- **Your servers.** The plan, the configuration, and the files you upload.
- **Billing.** Handled by Polar. They get your email and payment details; we
  get the subscription status and an invoice reference. We never see your card
  number.
- **Server logs.** Addresses and request metadata, to spot abuse and diagnose
  faults. Kept 14 days.
- **Activity log.** Consequential actions on your account, with who, when and
  from what address. Kept two years.

## Your files

We do not read them, except when you ask us to help with a specific problem —
and then only what is needed for that problem, by a person, recorded in your
[activity log](../account/account-activity-log.md).

We do not train anything on them. We do not sell anything. There is no
analytics product built on your data, because we do not have one.

## Where it lives

The EU. Our machines are in Germany; our database and mail providers are EU
based.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you can see it, take it, correct it and delete it.

- **See and take:** [export your data](../account/export-your-data.md),
  self-service and immediate.
- **Delete:** [delete your account](../account/delete-your-account.md),
  self-service and immediate. No grace period — read that article before
  pressing it.
- **Correct:** most of it in the dashboard; the rest by ticket.

You do not need to explain why, and asking costs nothing.

## Are you a processor for me?

If your bot handles your users' personal data, you are the controller and we
are your processor for what sits on our disks.

Need a data processing agreement? Open a ticket. Say who the counterparty is
and what your compliance team needs.

## Cookies

Session cookies so you stay signed in, and a stored preference for the cookie
banner itself. No advertising trackers. No third-party analytics following you
around.

## Still stuck?

[Open a ticket](https://shardly.xyz/app/support/new), or write to the address
in the [privacy policy](https://shardly.xyz/privacy) for formal requests.
