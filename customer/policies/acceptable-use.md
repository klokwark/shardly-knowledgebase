# Acceptable use

Shardly is for running Discord bots. This is what we will not host.

## Never

- **Cryptocurrency mining.** In any form, including "just when idle". It is
  the single most common abuse of bot hosting and we suspend for it without
  discussion.
- **Attacking other systems.** Denial of service, port scanning, brute force,
  credential stuffing — as a target or as a relay.
- **Distributing malware**, including bots whose purpose is to spread it.
- **Content that is illegal where we are.** We are in Germany and subject to
  German and EU law. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the
  authorities, immediately and without warning you.
- **Bots built to harass.** Mass DMs, raid tools, doxxing, stalking.
- **Breaking Discord's own terms.** Selfbots, user-token automation,
  engineered spam. If Discord would ban you for it, we will not host it.

## Not allowed without asking

- **Proxies, VPNs and tunnels.** Not because they are wrong, but because they
  make abuse from your server look like abuse from us.
- **Public file hosting.** A bot that stores its own files is fine; a bot that
  is a file host for strangers is a different product.
- **Heavy sustained load unrelated to a bot.** Video transcoding, model
  training, mass scraping. Ask and we will usually say yes to a reasonable
  case.

## Resources

Use what your plan gives you. You do not need to be frugal — you bought it.

What is not fine is deliberately trying to take more than you bought, or
finding a way around a limit and using it.

## What happens

It depends on what it is.

- **Something ambiguous:** we ask.
- **Clear abuse:** the server is suspended and we tell you why.
- **Illegal content:** suspended immediately, reported where the law requires
  it, no notice.

Suspension is not deletion. If it was a misunderstanding, tell us and we will
look again — we have got it wrong before and we would rather be corrected than
be right.

## Reporting somebody

If a Shardly-hosted bot is being used against you, open a ticket or email
abuse@shardly.xyz. Include the bot's name and tag, what it did, and when. We
take it seriously and we will not tell them who reported it.
