# What we keep, and for how long

| What | How long | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account and servers | While you have an account | To provide the service |
| Server files | While the server exists | They are yours |
| Backups | Per plan: 14 or 30 days, or until you delete them | |
| Server logs | 14 days | Abuse detection and fault diagnosis |
| Activity log | 2 years | Security, and settling disputes |
| Support tickets | Kept, anonymised after account deletion | Our record of the conversation |
| Invoices | 10 years | German tax law |
| Ticket attachments | With the ticket; unsent uploads swept after a day | |
| Delivered notifications | 14 days | To answer "did that ever send?" |

## When you delete your account

Immediately gone: the account, its sign-in methods, its sessions, every server
and every file.

**There is no grace period.** We cannot undo it. See
[delete your account](../account/delete-your-account.md).

Two things survive, and both are obligations rather than choices:

- **Invoices**, for ten years, because tax law requires it.
- **Support tickets**, with your name and address removed, as our record of
  what was said.

Server logs age out on their own within 14 days.

## Why the activity log is two years

Long enough to answer a dispute about something from last billing year. Short
enough to be a policy rather than a promise to keep everything forever.

It is append-only — nothing, including us, can edit or remove an entry. That
is what makes it worth anything when there is a disagreement.

## Backups

A backup lives with the server. **When a server is removed, its backups go
with it**, and we cannot get them back.

If a backup matters, download it. This matters most when you cancel.
